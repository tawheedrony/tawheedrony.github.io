Research

Topics include out-of-distribution detection, test-time adaptation, and edge AI. * denotes first author. Click a figure to enlarge it. Some papers are highlighted.

Mosaic: Improving Out-of-Distribution Detection in Vision Transformers with Patch Representations
Md Tawheedul Islam Bhuian*, Md Farhan Sadiq, Rahim Hossain, Kyoung-Don Kang
Under Review, 2026
project page

Global penultimate-layer features can miss OOD evidence carried by patch tokens. Mosaic pools patch tokens into an image descriptor, models its class-conditional geometry, and combines Mahalanobis distances from the global and patch feature spaces.

LSF: Layer Selection and Fusion for Post-Hoc Out-of-Distribution Detection
Rahim Hossain*, Md Tawheedul Islam Bhuian*, Md Farhan Sadiq, Kyoung-Don Kang
Under Review, 2026

The penultimate layer is tuned for class separation and can hide low-level signs of distribution shift. LSF selects the intermediate layer with the largest entropy-density drop and fuses it with the penultimate layer for post-hoc OOD detection.

SYNERGY: Post-Hoc LLM-Generated Text Detection via Syntactic Embedding and Relational Geometry
Md Tawheedul Islam Bhuian*, Md Farhan Sadiq, Kyoung-Don Kang
Under Review, 2026

SYNERGY models the class-conditional Gaussian geometry of hidden states for each syntactic dependency relation and aggregates relation-level scores into a post-hoc detector of LLM-generated text.

FATE: Pillar Encoding and Frequency-Aware Training for Event-Based Object Detection
Md Tawheedul Islam Bhuian*, Kyoung-Don Kang
arXiv, 2026
arXiv

Fixed temporal sub-bins discard fine timing in sparse event streams. FATE encodes spatial pillars with a Legendre polynomial basis and trains with temporally dense pseudo-labels for high-frequency detection.

TDA-L: Reducing Latency and Memory Consumption of Test-Time Adaptation for Real-Time Intelligent Sensing
Rahim Hossain, Md Tawheedul Islam Bhuian, Kyoung-Don Kang
Sensors, 2025
paper

Gradient-based test-time adaptation is too heavy for real-time sensing. TDA-L adds low-rank (LoRA) projections to the training-free TDA adapter to shrink the features it processes at test time.

Shared Encoder with Attention for Non-Intrusive Appliance Load Monitoring
Md Tawheedul Islam Bhuian*, Sadequal Islam, Md Forkan Uddin
IEEE ICPS, 2023
paper

Non-intrusive load monitoring infers each appliance's power from a single aggregate meter signal. A shared 1-D convolutional encoder with attention feeds separate regression and on/off classification heads.

Automated Level Crossing System - A Computer Vision Based Approach with Raspberry Pi Microcontroller
Rafid Umayer Murshed, Sandip Kollol Dhruba, Md. Tawheedul Islam Bhuian, Mst. Rumi Akter
ICECE, 2022
paper

Unmanned level crossings cause frequent accidents in Bangladesh. A low-cost Raspberry Pi system detects approaching trains from live video, closes the crossing, monitors for trespassers and alerts authorities.
